SPACE BY THE NUMBERS

Mind-bending statistics that challenge the limits of human perception.

VACUUM VELOCITY
299,792

KILOMETERS PER SECOND

The universal speed limit of light in a vacuum. At this velocity, you could circle Earth’s equator seven and a half times in a single second.

GRAVITATIONAL MONSTERS
66 BILLION

SOLAR MASSES (TON 618)

The mass of TON 618, one of the most gargantuan ultramassive black holes ever detected. Its event horizon spans nearly 390 billion km.

STELLAR EXPANSION
73.3

KM/S PER MEGAPARSEC

The Hubble Expansion Constant measuring the accelerating rate at which dark energy is stretching the fabric of spacetime apart.

EXTREME THERMODYNAMICS
15,000,000°C

CORE TEMPERATURE OF SOL

The searing temperature in the solar core where quantum tunneling allows protons to fuse against electrostatic repulsion.

ABSOLUTE ZERO FRONTIER
2.725 K

CMB RESIDUAL TEMPERATURE

The average thermal background temperature of deep intergalactic vacuum, warmed only by the faint afterglow of the Big Bang.

STELLAR POPULATION
2 TRILLION

TOTAL GALAXIES

Estimated number of galaxies in the entire observable sphere of the universe, each housing roughly 100 to 400 billion stars.

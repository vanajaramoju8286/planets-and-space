CORONAL MASS EJECTIONS

SOLAR FLARES & SPACE WEATHER

Magnetic reconnection events on the photosphere accelerate billions of tons of magnetized plasma across the solar system at speeds exceeding 3,000 km/s, triggering geomagnetic auroras across planetary magnetospheres.

GRAVITATIONAL EQUILIBRIUM

HYDROSTATIC BALANCE

The Sun maintains a delicate stability where outward radiation pressure generated by core hydrogen fusion exactly matches the colossal inward crush of its own gravity—a stable dance lasting 10 billion years.

THE HELIOSPHERE BOUNDARY

THE TERMINATION SHOCK

Where the supersonic solar wind slows below the speed of sound before colliding with interstellar cosmic rays. This bubble protects life on Earth by deflecting over 70% of lethal galactic radiation.

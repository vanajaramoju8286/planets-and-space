The Red Planet. Home to towering shield volcanoes, massive canyon rifts, and subsurface frozen brine.

Jupiter

Colossal gas giant with more mass than all other planets combined, exerting immense gravitational shield effects.

Distinguished by an elaborate ring system and 146 discovered moons, including ocean world Enceladus and hazy Titan.

Year: 29.4 Earth yrs
Moons: 146

Uranus

An ice giant tilted nearly 98 degrees on its rotational axis, rolling around the Sun like a cosmic bowling ball.

Diameter: 50,724 km
Year: 84 Earth yrs
Moons: 28

Neptune

Dark, cold, and whipped by supersonic winds. Orbiting more than 4.5 billion km from the warmth of the Sun.

Diameter: 49,244 km

SPACE MISSIONS ARCHIVE

From the first lunar boot-prints to robotic probes cruising through interstellar space.

ACTIVE ARCHIVES // 6 HISTORIC LOGS
1969 // NASA SUCCESS

APOLLO 11

Target: Mare Tranquillitatis, The Moon

Humanity's first manned lunar landing. Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin spent 21 hours on the lunar surface while Michael Collins piloted Columbia in orbit, returning 21.55 kg of lunar samples to Earth.

CREW: Armstrong, Aldrin, Collins | DURATION: 195h 18m
1977 // NASA JPL INTERSTELLAR

VOYAGER 1 & 2

Target: Outer Planets & Interstellar Space

Grand Tour of Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. In 2012, Voyager 1 became the first human-made object to cross the heliopause into interstellar space, now over 24 billion km from Earth.

PAYLOAD: Golden Record | SPEED: 61,200 km/h
1990 // NASA ESA OPERATIONAL

HUBBLE TELESCOPE

Target: Low Earth Orbit (547 km)

Revolutionized modern astrophysics with over 1.5 million observations. Resolved the expansion rate of the universe, discovered dark energy, and captured the iconic Ultra Deep Field.

MIRROR: 2.4m Primary | ORBITS: 180,000+
2021 // NASA ESA CSA ACTIVE SCIENCE

JAMES WEBB (JWST)

Target: Sun-Earth L2 Lagrange Point (1.5M km)

The premier infrared space observatory. Unfolded a 6.5m gold-beryllium primary mirror to detect the first primordial galaxies born just 300 million years after the Big Bang.

TEMPERATURE: -233°C (40 K) | WAVELENGTH: 0.6 - 28 μm
2020 // NASA JPL ACTIVE SAMPLING

PERSEVERANCE & INGENUITY

Target: Jezero Crater, Mars

Investigating ancient river deltas for biosignatures, collecting rock core sample tubes for future Earth return, and achieving the first powered aerial flights on another planet with Ingenuity.

FLIGHTS: 72 Aerial Sorties | CORES: 24 Sealed Tubes
2022+ // INTERNATIONAL ACTIVE CAMPAIGN

ARTEMIS PROGRAM

Target: Lunar South Pole & Lunar Gateway

Landing the first woman and first person of color on the Moon. Establishing sustainable long-term lunar surface infrastructure and testing technologies for crewed expeditions to Mars.

ROCKET: Space Launch System (SLS) | CRAFT: Orion Capsule

CHRONICLES OF EXISTENCE

FROM DUST TO DISCOVERY

Interactive chronological era stepper tracking the 13.8 billion-year journey of matter.

13.8 BILLION B.C. // COSMIC DAWN

The Big Bang

Spacetime, gravity, and the fundamental forces expand infinitely from an incomprehensibly dense and searing quantum singularity. Matter and antimatter clash until a minute baryon asymmetry seeds all atoms.

Cosmic Microwave Background radiation confirms universal expansion.
13.6 BILLION B.C. // REIONIZATION

First Primordial Stars

Population III hyper-massive stars ignite in dense hydrogen clouds, fusing light gases into carbon, oxygen, and iron before detonating in cataclysmic pair-instability supernovae.

Seeded the barren cosmos with the building blocks of planets.
4.6 BILLION B.C. // ACCRETION DISK

Solar System Birth

A molecular cloud collapses in the Orion Spur of the Milky Way, condensing into our Sun and a swirling accretion disk where electrostatic grains collide to form planetesimals.

99.86% of total system mass condensed into our home star.
3.8 BILLION B.C. // BIOSPHERE DAWN

Earliest Terran Life

Single-celled extremophiles awaken around deep-sea hydrothermal vents in primordial volcanic oceans. Cyanobacteria begin the Great Oxidation Event, shielding the planet with ozone.

The emergence of organic molecular self-replication.
1969 A.D. // SPACE AGE

First Lunar Footstep

Apollo 11 touches down on the Sea of Tranquility. Humanity sets physical boot-prints on another celestial world for the first time in planetary history.

"One small step for man, one giant leap for mankind."
2022 A.D. // DEEP INFRARED

JWST Cosmic Dawn

James Webb Space Telescope unfurls its 6.5-meter gold mirror at L2, capturing the earliest proto-galaxies ever imaged and analyzing exoplanetary atmospheres for water vapor.

Resolving light emitted just 300 million years after the Big Bang.
THE FUTURE // MULTI-PLANETARY

Interstellar Frontier

Permanent surface laboratories on the Moon and Mars, followed by robotic lightsail micro-probes launched toward Alpha Centauri at 20% the speed of light.

The dawn of an interstellar multi-planetary civilization.
